DEFENCE minister

Defence minister of India Rajnath Singh on Saturday said that the country is ready to face any challenge and will not compromise on its security. He was speaking at the 75th anniversary of the Indian Air Force in New Delhi. Singh said that the IAF has always been at the forefront of defending the nation and has played a crucial role in safeguarding India's borders. He also highlighted the importance of modernizing the armed forces and investing in advanced technology to enhance their capabilities.
